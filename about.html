About this tool

About Gematria Calculator

Gematria Calculator is an independent reference site for calculating symbolic word, name, phrase, and Hebrew letter values with clear method labels and transparent breakdowns.

Our Purpose

This website was created for people who want a fast, readable way to calculate gematria values and understand how each total was formed. The goal is simple: show the method, show the letter values, and help users compare results without hiding the calculation behind a black box.

Gematria is used in many ways, including language study, symbolic reflection, spiritual journaling, creative writing, name comparison, and historical curiosity. This site supports those uses while keeping the results clearly labeled as interpretive, not predictive.

What We Offer

English Gematria Calculator

Calculate English values using the A=6, B=12, C=18 sequence and review each letter contribution.

Reverse Gematria Calculator

Compare words with a reversed alphabet system where Z begins the sequence and A closes it.

Name Gematria Calculator

Study first names, full names, initials, and spelling variants while keeping the exact input visible.

Hebrew Gematria Calculator

Calculate standard Hebrew letter values, including common final letter forms used in Hebrew words.

Editorial Approach

Every calculator page is written to explain what the method does before asking users to trust the result. Articles and number meaning pages are designed as educational guides: they define terms, give examples, explain limitations, and encourage users to verify spelling and method choices.

When we publish or revise content, we focus on clarity, original explanations, useful examples, and responsible language. We avoid presenting symbolic number meanings as guaranteed facts, secret proof, medical advice, legal advice, financial advice, or life decision guidance.

Accuracy and Review

The calculators run in the browser and are checked against known sample words and letter tables. Results can still vary when users change spelling, punctuation, transliteration, language, final Hebrew forms, or the calculator method. For that reason, each serious comparison should include the exact input, method, total, and letter breakdown.

If you notice an incorrect value, unclear explanation, broken link, typo, or missing context, please send the page URL and details through the contact page. Correction requests help keep the site useful for future visitors.

Transparency

Gematria Calculator is maintained as a public educational website. It does not require user accounts, and calculator input is processed in the browser. The site may use advertising, analytics, or third-party services as described in the Privacy Policy.

For questions, corrections, or editorial feedback, contact calculatorefy@gmail.com. Legal and usage details are available in the Terms and Conditions and Disclaimer.
