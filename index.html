What Is Gematria?

Gematria is a symbolic method of assigning numbers to letters. When those letter values are added together, a word, name, or phrase becomes a number that can be compared with other words or studied as a symbolic value.

Different gematria systems use different letter values. Simple gematria uses A=1 through Z=26. English gematria uses the same alphabet positions multiplied by 6, so A=6 and Z=156. Jewish gematria uses a special Latin-letter table with larger values for letters such as J, V, W, Y, and Z. Hebrew gematria uses Hebrew letter values, including common final forms.

This website is designed for people who want a clear, beginner-friendly way to calculate totals and understand possible meanings. The content is educational, symbolic, spiritual, and entertainment-focused. It does not claim that a number proves a hidden fact or predicts an outcome.

How to Calculate Gematria

Gematria calculation follows one basic idea: give every supported letter a value, then add those values together. The part that changes is the value table. In Simple gematria, A through Z are 1 through 26. In English gematria, those same alphabet positions are multiplied by 6. In Jewish gematria, several Latin letters use larger traditional-style values, so words can produce very different totals.

Capitalization does not change the value. For example, A and a are counted the same way. Spaces and punctuation are ignored, which means a phrase is calculated from its letters rather than from the marks between words.

Choosing a Gematria Method

Use English Gematria when you want the A=6 through Z=156 table. Use Simple Gematria when you want A=1 through Z=26. Use Jewish Gematria when you want the special Latin-letter values shown in the table. Reverse and reduction methods are still available for comparison, and Hebrew Gematria should be selected when your input is written in Hebrew letters.

No method on this site is presented as the only correct method. Each system answers a slightly different question. For consistent notes, choose one method for a set of comparisons, keep the spelling the same, and avoid mixing results from different systems unless the comparison itself is the point. That simple discipline makes gematria more readable and more honest.

Gematria Calculator FAQ

What is a Gematria Calculator?

A Gematria Calculator assigns number values to letters and adds them to calculate the total for a word, name, or phrase.

How does English gematria work?

English gematria uses six-step values: A=6, B=12, C=18, and so on through Z=156.

What is reverse gematria?

Reverse gematria flips the English alphabet values so A is 26, B is 25, and Z is 1.

Can I calculate my name value?

Yes. Enter a first name, full name, nickname, or initials and choose a method to see a symbolic number value.

Does this calculator support Hebrew gematria?

Yes. The Hebrew method supports standard Hebrew letter values and common final letter forms.

Is gematria the same as numerology?

They are related symbolic systems, but gematria focuses on letter values while numerology often studies dates and personal number patterns.

Are gematria number meanings always the same?

No. Number meanings are interpretive and may vary by tradition, culture, and personal context.
