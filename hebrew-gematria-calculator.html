What Is Hebrew Gematria?

Hebrew gematria assigns numerical values to Hebrew letters. The traditional sequence begins with aleph as 1, bet as 2, and continues through higher tens and hundreds.

This calculator focuses on standard Hebrew values used by many beginner references. It supports common final forms with their standard values and is meant as a clear starting point.

Hebrew Gematria Calculator FAQ

Does this support final Hebrew letters?

Yes. Final kaf, mem, nun, pe, and tsadi are supported with their common standard values.

Can I enter Hebrew phrases?

Yes. Supported Hebrew letters are counted and spaces or punctuation are ignored.

Does this include every historical Hebrew method?

No. This calculator focuses on standard values for common use and beginner learning.

Can I mix English and Hebrew text?

For clear results, select the method that matches your text. Latin-letter methods count English letters, and the Hebrew method counts Hebrew letters.

Reading Hebrew Gematria Results

Hebrew gematria should be read with attention to spelling and letter forms. The same English transliteration can represent different Hebrew spellings, so it is best to enter the Hebrew letters directly when possible. The calculator counts supported Hebrew letters and ignores punctuation or spaces.

This tool uses standard values and common final-letter handling, which makes it suitable for quick checks and beginner study. It is not a substitute for tradition-specific scholarship, grammar, or context. If a result matters for serious study, verify the spelling and compare the calculation with a trusted reference.

Hebrew Spelling Notes

For clearer Hebrew results, enter the word as it is normally written and avoid relying on English transliteration. Transliteration can vary from one source to another, while the calculator needs the actual Hebrew letters to apply the correct values.

Responsible Calculator Use

Gematria works best when the calculator supports your thinking rather than replacing it. Use the result as a way to notice patterns, compare spellings, or organize symbolic notes. If a number feels meaningful, slow down and check the breakdown before drawing conclusions. The letters, language, method, and context all matter.

For study notes, record the exact text, the selected method, and the date of the calculation. This makes your comparisons easier to repeat and keeps the interpretation clear. Avoid using any gematria value as advice about money, health, law, religion, relationships, or major life decisions.
